## HASHING
* Hash is a mathematical algorithm to convert data into number for the sake of safety, if the data is slightly changed, the hash will be completly changed
* SHA - secure hash algorithm, SHA-3 is the highest level of security, used for password storage, secure communication
* MD5 - was widely used but now considered not as secure due to known vulnerabilities
* HMAC - hash based message authentication code, combines has function to verify authenticity and integerity
* CRC - cyclic redundancy check, for error checking

* Load balancing - distributing traffice across multiple servers
* Clustering - groups multiple servers together to act as single system
* Failover - automatic process of redirecting operations to a system when the primary system encounters outage
* Parallel processing - splits tasks into smaller subtaks and executes them
* Hot site - duplicated system with real time data
* Warm site - pre-configred with basic software/hardware, partial data backups
* Cold site - empty physical space, power and basic utilities with no hardware/software
* COOP - continuity of opererations plan, federal plan to ensure essential functions conitnue during emergencies
* Tabletop exercise - walkthrough of incident scnerios

## RISK MANAGEMENT CONCETPS
* Risk identification, analysis and assessment
* Ad hoc - assessment triggered by specific events
* Recurring - conducted on a set schedule
* One time - specific project
* Continuous - real time, ongoing evalutation 
 * Qualitative risk assessment - evaluates threat probability and impact using subjective judgment
 * Quantitative risk assessment - uses numerical values to evaluate risk
 * SLE - single loss expectancy, predicted loss value to an asset from a single incident, Asset value (AV) x Exposure factor (EF)
 * ARO - annualized rate of occurance, how often a threat exploits the system
 * ALE - annualized loss expectancy, annual financial loss due to risk
 * ALE = ARO x SLE
 * Risk tranference - transferring the risk to third party
 * Risk avoidance - eliminating exposure to a risk
 * Risk Mitigation - active measures to reduce risk
 * Risk acceptance - tolerating the risk
 * Risk appetite - attitude and willingness to take on risk to achieve objectives
 * RTO - recovery time objective, max allowable time to restore critical business functions after disruption
 * RPO - recovery point objective, max acceptable data loss measured in time prior to an outage
 * MTTR - mean time to repair
 * MTBF - mean time between failures
 * MTTF - mean time to failure
